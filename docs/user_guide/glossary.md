## Glossary

**cents**
$1200\log_2 \frac{\mathrm{one\ frequency}}{\mathrm{another\ frequency}}$. 1200¢ is one octave.

**edo**
equally divided octave. p-edo means that the octave is divided into p steps of equally many cents. p-ed-q means that the ratio q is dividided into p equal steps.

**Halberstadt**
the 7 white + 5 black key layout developed for organs. Also used for pianos, accordions, and synthesisers.

**isomorphic**
any layout where the same chord shape represents the same chord regardless of how you move it.
